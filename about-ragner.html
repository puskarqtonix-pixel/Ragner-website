ABOUT RAGNER

A founder-led digital marketing agency from Kolkata.

Ragner helps businesses build a clearer digital presence through SEO, website development, local SEO, paid advertising, social media marketing and answer-focused content.

What does Ragner do?

Ragner works on the parts of digital marketing that help businesses become easier to discover, understand and trust. That includes search engine optimization, local visibility, website structure and messaging, paid campaigns and useful content.

SEO & Local SEO

Improve search visibility with technical SEO, on-page optimization, content and local search foundations.

Web Development

Create responsive business websites with clear messaging and strong technical foundations.

Performance Marketing

Use paid channels and focused landing pages to reach people with stronger intent.

Who does Ragner serve?

Ragner is designed for businesses that want a more structured online presence, including local businesses, service providers, startups and growing companies that need stronger visibility and clearer customer journeys.
