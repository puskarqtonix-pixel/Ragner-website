05 / ANSWERS

Questions businesses ask.

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency helps businesses attract and convert customers through online channels such as search engines, websites, social media, email and paid advertising. Ragner focuses on SEO, websites, local SEO, paid advertising, social media and AI-search visibility.

What is SEO and how can it help a business?

Search engine optimization (SEO) improves the technical quality, relevance and usefulness of a website so search engines can better understand its pages. Good SEO can help a business earn more relevant organic visibility over time.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the process of improving a business's visibility for searches connected to a location. It can include Google Business Profile optimization, location-focused pages, accurate business information and locally relevant content.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is a practical approach to making information clear, useful and easy for answer-oriented search experiences to understand. It includes answering real customer questions, demonstrating expertise and structuring information clearly.

How long does SEO take?

SEO timelines vary by competition, website condition, target queries, content quality, authority and the market. SEO is generally a longer-term process rather than an instant result.

Does Ragner work with businesses outside Kolkata?

Yes. Ragner can work remotely with businesses in other cities and regions while tailoring the strategy to the business's target market.

Can Ragner build a website from scratch?

Yes. Ragner can build a responsive business website with clear messaging, technical SEO foundations, mobile-friendly design and conversion-focused calls to action.
