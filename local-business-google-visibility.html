How can a local business improve visibility on Google?

Local visibility starts with accurate business information, a useful website and a well-maintained Google Business Profile.

Complete your business information

Keep business name, category, contact details, hours and service information accurate.

Build useful service and location content

Create pages that genuinely explain services and areas served rather than duplicating city names.

Earn genuine reviews and mentions

Customer reviews build trust. Relevant local mentions and legitimate links can also improve discovery.
